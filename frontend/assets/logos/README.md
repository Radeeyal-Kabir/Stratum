# Company identity assets

These marks identify the companies covered by the screener; they do not imply endorsement.
The source and company URL for each asset are recorded in `sources.json`.

SVG marks are from Simple Icons v11.15.0 (CC0-1.0). Brand trademarks remain
the property of their respective owners. PNGs are the companies' favicons,
retrieved at 128px through Google's favicon service. Assets are stored locally
so site visitors make no requests to an external logo service.
